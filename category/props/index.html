About these props models

Every prop model here is a glTF 2.0 binary (.glb) with its textures inside, ready for three.js, Babylon.js, Unity, Unreal, Godot, Blender and AR/VR. They are free for personal and commercial projects — the license travels inside each file, and models that ask for credit name their author on the model page. Licensing details
