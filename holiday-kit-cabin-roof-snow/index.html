About the Cabin Roof Snow 3D model

Cabin Roof Snow is a very low-poly prop model with 116 triangles and 214 vertices. It measures 1.31 × 1.48 × 1.40 m in real-world units and uses 1 texture and 1 material, all packed into one 12 KB .glb file.

It is model 19 of 99 in the Holiday Kit collection, so it shares a scale and art style with the rest of that set — you can mix them in one scene or download the whole collection as a single pack.

It is a static (unrigged) mesh, ready to place in a scene as-is in Blender, Unity, Unreal, Godot, three.js or Babylon.js.

At this triangle count it suits mobile games, WebGL pages and scenes that place hundreds of copies. Typical uses: dressing game levels, dungeons and adventure maps, modular kit building, scene detail.

Released under CC0 1.0 — public domain: free for personal and commercial projects.
